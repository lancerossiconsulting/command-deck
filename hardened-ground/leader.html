How to Use These Outlines

Each session follows the same shape: Setting, Context, Scripture, Reflection, then For Discussion. Begin with the opening question. The Reflection movements suggest a teaching flow. Use your own words. Leave room for the text to interrupt you, and for the room to talk. Close with the takeaway. Hebrews 3:15 runs through every week. Week 8 ends with John 20:21 and the question of what being sent looks like in the twenty-first century.

Closing the course. Leave the last spoken word as John 20:21: “As the Father has sent me, so I am sending you.”
