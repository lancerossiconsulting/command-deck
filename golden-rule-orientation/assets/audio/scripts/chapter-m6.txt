# Matilda script for m6-medication-reporting
# Voice: ElevenLabs Matilda
# Target file: public/assets/audio/chapter-m6.mp3

If you see a medication concern or missed dose, call the manager right away.

Never attempt to correct or alter medications yourself. Always remain within your supportive care scope.

If you suspect abuse, neglect, or financial exploitation, act right away. Call 911 for danger. For agency-related concerns, call K-DADS at 1-800-842-0078, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. After hours or for someone outside the agency, call the Kansas DCF 24-hour hotline. You do not need agency permission before making a report. File your report, then notify the administrator the same day.
