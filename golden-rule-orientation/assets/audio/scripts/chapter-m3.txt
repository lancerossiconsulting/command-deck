Check your schedule spreadsheet before each shift.

If you are going to be late or cannot work, call the administrator at seven eight five, three two three, zero one zero four as soon as you know.

For predictable absences, give at least 24 hours notice. For an unplanned call-out, aim for 4 to 6 hours. Two hours is the absolute minimum. If a sudden problem leaves less time, call right away. Missing a shift without calling results in a final warning.
