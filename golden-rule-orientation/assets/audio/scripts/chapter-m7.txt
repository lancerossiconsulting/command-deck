Professional boundaries protect you and our clients.

Small tokens under ten dollars, like a seasonal card or cup of coffee, are acceptable. Decline all cash, loans, and valuable gifts every time.

If a client or family asks you to take a photo, check with the administrator first. Do not take or share unauthorized images of clients or their homes. Marketing use needs a signed Section N release. Refer thrift, donation, or resale requests to the administrator.
